# Evergreen Mode — Locations (A–E)

Transcribed from photographs of the physical cards (John, 2026-09-06). **Reference only — none of this
is implemented.** See [EVERGREEN-BOONS.md](EVERGREEN-BOONS.md) and
[EVERGREEN-BANES.md](EVERGREEN-BANES.md).

A Location is the scenario's **mission-rules card** — it carries SETUP, START OF TURN, END OF TURN and
ENEMY DEFEATED rules of its own, the same shape a campaign mission does. It is not a theme or a
backdrop.

**The letter in the card's top-right corner is the scenario code's prefix.** `HILLS = D` and
`LAKE = E` are confirmed from the photo, which fixes the box order the other three follow:

| Letter | Location | Confirmed |
|---|---|---|
| A | Shrine | inferred from box order |
| B | Cave | inferred |
| C | Forest | inferred |
| **D** | **Hills** | ✅ photographed |
| **E** | **Lake** | ✅ photographed |

So the published scenario `E-2-3-10-22-29-33` is a **Lake** scenario, and `D-1-7-12-16-27-31` a
**Hills** one.

## D — Hills

> **END OF TURN**
> Put the top card of the banished pile into the mission zone. (**B** → **MZ**)
>
> Players suffer **team damage** equal to the combined strength of cards in the mission zone (satisfy
> this damage using the same rules as team damage in mission 6). If the damage can't be satisfied the
> game is lost.

> **ENEMY DEFEATED**
> Put the enemy and cards in mission zone into the banished pile then shuffle the banished pile. If
> exact damage was dealt, you may first discard one card from the mission zone.
>
> Iconography: 💀 (exact) = **MZ** → **D**; then **MZ** → **B**.

## E — Lake

> **SETUP**
> Shuffle pilgrims and place in pilgrim deck in mission zone.

> **START OF TURN**
> Put the top card of the pilgrim deck (or reserve deck if pilgrim deck is empty) into the mission
> zone. (**P** → **MZ**)

> **ENEMY DEFEATED**
> For each card in the mission zone, banish a card from reserve deck, then move the cards in the
> mission zone to the discard pile in any order. Then banish the enemy, or discard it if exact damage
> was dealt.
>
> Iconography: **R** → **B** (once per mission-zone card); **MZ** → **D**.

## What these confirm

- **Locations reuse campaign machinery wholesale.** Hills is Mission 12's escalating banish-pile-into-
  the-zone loop with Mission 6's team damage bolted on; Lake is Mission 8's pilgrim deck. Evergreen
  Mode recombines mission mechanics rather than inventing new ones — which is why the E-box is
  described as a mission generator.
- **The exact-kill distinction runs through the whole mode**, exactly as it does in the campaign:
  both Locations here branch on whether exact damage was dealt, as do banes 15 and boon 33.
- **"Banish the enemy" is a real step**, matching John's 2026-09-06 ruling for Mission 11 and 12 that
  a defeated enemy's own card goes to the banish pile.
