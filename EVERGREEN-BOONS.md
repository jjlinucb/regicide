# Evergreen Mode — Boons (24–35)

Transcribed from photographs of the physical cards (John, 2026-09-06). **Reference only — none of this
is implemented.** See [EVERGREEN-BANES.md](EVERGREEN-BANES.md) and
[EVERGREEN-LOCATIONS.md](EVERGREEN-LOCATIONS.md).

Boons are numbered **24–35** (12 cards). A published scenario code is one Location plus four banes
plus two boons, e.g. `C-4-8-15-19-25-32`.

Each card carries a timing band naming when it applies: **SETUP**, **SPECIAL RULES**,
**ENEMY DEFEATED**, or **END OF TURN**.

---

## 25 — There Are Two Of Them!

> **SETUP** — Add Gøran the Evergreen to your reserve deck (from mission box 10).

## 26 — Best Friend

> **SETUP** — Add the puppy (from mission box 8) to the reserve deck.
>
> **SPECIAL RULES** — When you play the puppy it has all four base classes for this attack (cleric,
> bard, warrior, paladin).

## 27 — Too Easy!

> **SPECIAL RULES** — The hand size limit is increased by 1.

## 29 — Second Breakfast

> **SETUP** — Add Oaken (3 ♣), Tolman (3 🪶) and [*third name obscured in the photo*] to the reserve
> deck (from mission boxes 5, 7 and 8 respectively).

## 33 — Restored Arrows

> **ENEMY DEFEATED** — If exact damage was dealt, you may put any one card from the banished pile to
> the bottom of the reserve deck.
>
> Iconography: 💀 (exact kill) = **B** → **R** (under).

## 35 — A Close Call

> **SPECIAL RULES** — When you satisfy damage for the exact amount you suffered (not including team
> damage or when damage is 0), place the discarded cards on the bottom of the reserve deck instead of
> the discard pile, then search the discard pile and put a card from it on top of the reserve deck.
