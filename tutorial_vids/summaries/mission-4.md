# Mission 4 — Fusion of Darkness

## Story
A trusted ally reveals his true colors, and the party has to defend something called "the evergreen" from a wave of monstrous beasts.

## How it plays / special rules
Confirmed by a full solo playthrough (Meet Me at the Table, "Mission 4 Playthrough" — three attempts, the third a win):

- Standard 4-4-4 escalating lineup: four enemies at 10 strength/20 health, four at 15/30, four at 20/40 (matches this codebase's tier stats exactly).
- **Discard-pile buff:** whatever card currently sits on top of the discard pile adds its value straight onto the active enemy's attack. This is snapshotted once per turn — you take the buffed damage, choose which cards to discard to cover it in one action, and only *after* those cards land does the new top card apply to the *next* enemy. It doesn't retroactively change the damage you're currently paying down.
- **Exact kills** go to the top of the reserve deck instead of the discard pile; any other kill's played cards go to the discard pile as normal (feeding the buff above).
- **Corrupted return queue:** a defeated enemy isn't gone — it rejoins the back of the 12-enemy fight queue once, corrupted (ignores immunity, its own play banishes the reserve deck's top card), then stays gone for good if defeated again. The video calls this out up front as a general warning before the fight starts, not something that hits an enemy's first appearance.
- The playthrough confirms this is one of the hardest missions in the campaign — the host lost twice before winning on the third attempt, specifically because of how the discard-buff and corrupted-requeue mechanics compound over a long fight.
- Cosmetic note: the physical cards name these enemies "Subject N, Phase I/II/III Experiment" rather than this codebase's invented "Specimen NN-Letter: The Whatever" flavor names — stats line up exactly, only the names differ, so no code change implied.

## Rewards (two relics/rewards this mission)
- **Gøran joins the party** — introduced in this mission's own ending, as a basic rank-8 card (no suit revealed yet, described as getting "much cooler later"). See "Not yet implemented" below.
- **Beast companions (x4):** tied to a specific character, these work like Animal Companions — paired with one other card — but instead of adding a flat +1 strength, a beast *copies* the strength of the card it's paired with (worth 0 alone). The video's own framing of the reward is "keep the four beasts in a box next to your party; each mission attempt you may include *one* of the four in your reserve deck" — i.e. a per-attempt pick from a shared pool of four, not all four merged permanently into the party's decks at once. See "Not yet implemented" below.
- **Relic — Scarlet Whistle:** opens whenever a player attacks alone with a lone Animal/Beast Companion. In co-op, any other player may silently add one card from their hand to the attack before it resolves. The video separately confirms a **solo-specific variant** the host called out as something not commonly shown: in solo play, the lone attacker instead pulls the top card of the discard pile and folds its value into the attack. See "Not yet implemented" below.

### Not yet implemented / worth a second look
1. **Scarlet Whistle has no solo behavior at all.** `engine.ts`'s `playCards` only opens the assist window when `state.players.length > 1` (`canOpenComboAssist`); in solo it just resolves the lone Companion attack with no bonus. Per this video, solo play should still get something — pulling the discard pile's top card into the attack — rather than the relic doing nothing. This mirrors the Kinfolk Flute's split solo/co-op behavior already implemented for Mission 1's relic.
2. **Gøran is missing from Mission 4's reward.** `packages/shared/src/legacy/missions.ts`'s Mission 4 `reward.recruits` currently only lists the 4 beast recruits (Fennow/Cressida/Orwick/Sabrielle) plus the `SCARLET_WHISTLE` relic — no Gøran card. This isn't a fresh discovery: a comment already sitting in that file (near Mission 8's entry, ~line 750–757) records that an earlier research pass found Gøran (rank 8, no suit) belongs here, but by the time that was found, Mission 4 had already been merged without him, so the team deferred him to Mission 8 as a known compromise. This playthrough directly confirms the original finding — Gøran's recruitment is one mission later than sourced material expects.
3. **Beast Companion reward structure may be modeled wrong.** Current code (`beastRecruit` in `missions.ts`, applied via `party.ts`'s `applyReward`) adds all 4 Beast Companions permanently into the campaign roster, same as any other recruit — available in every future mission's reserve deck from Mission 5 onward. The video's reward text instead describes a shared pool kept "in the box," with only *one* beast selectable per mission attempt. This would be a more invasive rework (it also touches Mission 11's `beastDeckMechanic`, which currently assumes all 4 beasts are already sitting in the permanent party) — flagging for review rather than treating as confirmed, since a single video's paraphrase of reward-card text is a thinner source than the mechanic-in-action confirmations above.
