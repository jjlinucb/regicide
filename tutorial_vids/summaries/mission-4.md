# Mission 4 — Fusion of Darkness

## Story
A trusted ally reveals his true colors, and the party has to defend something called "the evergreen" from a wave of monstrous beasts.

## How it plays / special rules
- Every enemy this mission gets bonus strength equal to whatever card currently sits on top of the discard pile — and that can shift mid-fight (e.g. a Cleric's heal reshuffles the discard pile, which changes the top card).
- Defeated enemies come back later in the fight queue as **corrupted** enemies, following the normal corrupted-card rules (banish a reserve card when played, ignore immunity).

## Rewards (two relics this mission)
- **Beast companions (x4):** tied to a specific character, these work like Animal Companions — paired with one other card — but instead of adding a flat +1 strength, a beast *copies* the strength of the card it's paired with.
- **Relic — Scarlet Whistle:** when a player attacks with a lone Animal Companion or Beast card, any other player may silently add one card from their hand to help the attack.
