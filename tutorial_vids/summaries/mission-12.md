# Mission 12 — Decay to Growth (Final Mission)

## Story
The party runs the campaign's antagonist to ground in his underground throne room, where he unleashes corrupted royalty as a last line of defense.

## How it plays / special rules
- A previous relic gets swapped for an upgraded version this mission: cards that were "corrupted" become "restored." A restored card ignores enemy immunity like a corrupted card does, but instead of banishing a reserve card when played, it *heals* the top card of the banish pile back into the game (returned under the reserve deck). Restored cards can never end up in the banish pile themselves — anywhere that would send one there redirects it to the bottom of the reserve deck instead.
- Corrupted cards get a special rule too: anywhere that would put one into the reserve deck instead sends it to the bottom of the banish pile.
- **Start of each turn:** the top card of the banish pile moves into the mission zone, where it adds both strength and immunity to the enemy.
- Defeating an enemy triggers a longer cleanup: banish the whole mission zone, then the enemy, then the entire discard pile (order preserved). An exact kill skips the start-of-turn effect on the first turn against the next enemy.

## Reward
The campaign's final mission — no further reward beyond completing the story.
