## Story
The campaign's mastermind confronts the party directly at the ashes of the mission-9 temple, twisting eight of the syndicate's own corrupted former members into the mission's enemy line.

## How it plays / special rules
- The enemies for this mission are eight corrupted party members, stacked weakest to strongest (lowest strength faced first, highest strength faced last). Each enemy's health is a fixed multiple (5x) of its strength.
- **Start of every turn:** the top card of the reserve deck flips face-up into a shared mission zone. Enemies draw bonus strength from everything sitting in that zone, so their effective strength climbs the longer a fight drags on.
- Enemies are immune to their own class as normal, but each class also grants the *enemy* an extra power no player version has:
  - Enemy Warriors double their total strength (base + mission-zone bonus) before any Paladin reduction is subtracted.
  - Enemy Paladins reduce the damage they take by their own base strength.
  - Enemy Clerics add an end-of-turn effect: the top discard-pile card moves into the mission zone.
  - Enemy Bards add an end-of-turn effect: the current player must move a card from hand into the mission zone (skipped if their hand is empty).
- Defeating an enemy skips end-of-turn effects that turn. The mission-zone cards go to the banish pile normally, or to the discard pile instead on an exact kill; the defeated enemy itself always goes to the discard pile either way.
