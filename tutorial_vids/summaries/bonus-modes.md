# Bonus modes

Two extra modes beyond the 12-mission campaign, relevant background for anything we build around replayability/difficulty.

## Evergreen Mode
A post-campaign replay mode built around the powerful deck a party has by the end of Mission 12 — directly comparable to the "Endless Mode" we already shipped this session (Kings joining the deck, escalating enemies). The real game's version: reuse the tougher back-half enemies from the mission-4 pool (re-sleeved as corrupted), pick a random location card, and draw a small set of modifier cards ("boons"/"banes" — complexity-tiered, not difficulty-tiered) that each add their own start/end-of-turn effects and mission-zone interactions. Defeated enemies can end up back in the party's own deck, still flagged corrupted when played. Framed explicitly as a sandbox to keep exploring after the story ends, not a fixed final challenge.

## Mercenaries
A rubber-band difficulty system: losing a mission earns "coins" (1 coin after the 1st loss, 2 after the 2nd, and so on, non-stacking — unspent coins don't carry over), which can hire temporary "mercenary" cards into the reserve deck for future attempts at that same mission. Mercenaries are returned once the mission is finally won, so they never become part of the permanent party. Some mercenary cards have a variable strength (declared when played, then fixed for as long as they stay on the tableau) or a chosen class (picked when they join an attack), adding a bit of flexible power to a struggling party without permanently unbalancing the campaign.
