# Mission 5 — High and Mighty

## Story
Chasing enemies to the first of "four veils," the party is swarmed by mutated fungal creatures in a grove.

## How it plays / special rules
- Four new "Reaver" party members join. A Reaver's power reveals a chosen number of cards off the top of the reserve deck and banishes them, adding one selected card's strength as bonus damage (not bonus defense/blocking — only the damage dealt). This bonus can stack with a Warrior's damage-doubling for very large hits.
- A rolling mission-zone/banish-pile cycle each turn feeds bonus strength to the current enemy.
- Defeating an enemy with exact damage carries bonus damage into the *next* fight, equal to the fallen enemy's base strength.

## Meta mechanics shown
- How to add a second class-icon sticker to an existing card.
- How to permanently retire cards from the party roster (used here to trim the new Reavers back down after the mission).
