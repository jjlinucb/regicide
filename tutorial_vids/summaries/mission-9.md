# Mission 9 — Hope from Ashes

## Story
The party defends a great, burning tree-temple from the campaign's recurring antagonist and her allies.

## How it plays / special rules
- Setup splits most of the party roster into three face-down piles as a shared mission zone, each with its top card revealed. A separate pool of Pilgrim cards is mixed into the reserve deck for this mission.
- At the end of your turn, you may banish a card from hand to "rescue" one of the revealed mission-zone cards into the discard pile (optional — declining just cycles that pile to its next card).
- An exact kill lets that rescue go straight to the top of the reserve deck instead of the discard pile.

## Rewards
- **Relic — Evergreen Mother:** softens the cost of playing a corrupted card. Instead of always banishing the top of the reserve deck, another player can instead pay the cost by banishing a card from their own hand.
- **A second Mage sticker:** one more eligible party member gains the Mage class.
- **A capstone class upgrade** (tied to the mission 4 beast character): a power that stacks all four base class effects at once — heal, draw, double damage, and reduce enemy strength — and notably ignores enemy immunity entirely.
