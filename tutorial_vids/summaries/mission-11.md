# Mission 11 — Descent into Darkness

## Story
The party's underground pursuit leads to a cavern where their old ally is found bound to a corrupting machine, guarded by four elite enemies.

## How it plays / special rules
- The mission-4 beast cards (normally a reward pool) are shuffled into a face-down deck sitting in the mission zone for this fight only — so no beast joins the active party this mission, though an unrelated Mage-aligned party member is still usable as normal.
- **Start of each turn:** flip the top card of that beast deck for a one-shot effect keyed to its class, e.g. a Warrior-flip banishes the top discard-pile card, a Paladin-flip discards the top reserve card, a Cleric-flip has the current player discard from hand, a Bard-flip has the current player banish from hand if possible. Once the deck runs out, it's reshuffled and the cycle continues.
- Separately, the enemy draws bonus strength *and* immunity from whatever cards currently sit on top of the discard pile and banish pile — so both piles are live threats to track, not just dead zones.
- Defeating the enemy always banishes it; an exact kill skips the beast-deck flip on the next turn.

## Reward
Winning lets the party pick one of the mission-4 beast cards to carry into Mission 12.
