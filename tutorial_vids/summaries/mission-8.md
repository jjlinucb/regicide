# Mission 8 — Winds of Chaos

## Story
A brief respite on windswept cliffs before the party has to deal with an incoming aerial threat.

## How it plays / special rules
- Four new "Chanter" party members join. A Chanter's power has every player draw a chosen number of cards all at once (even past the hand limit, discarding back down after) — like the Bard's draw, but simultaneous for the whole table instead of one card at a time around it.
- The Pilgrim cards return, this time shuffled permanently into the reserve deck. Defeating an enemy lets the party build an ascending "run" of cards into a mission zone (each new card exactly one strength higher than the last); enemies gain bonus strength from any non-Pilgrim cards sitting there. Building the run up to a strength-10 card triggers a one-time payoff: dump the zone to the discard pile, freely banish whatever you want from it, then reshuffle the rest to the bottom of the reserve deck.
