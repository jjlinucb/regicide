# Mission 7 — Tales of Rebirth

## Story
The party defends a sacred well from corrupted, machine-like threats emerging from the water.

## How it plays / special rules
- Four new "Druid" party members join. A Druid's power has the whole table set their hands aside, reshuffles the entire discard pile, and deals it back out — each player then sorts a few cards between the banish pile, their own hand, and the top/bottom of the reserve deck.
- A separate pool of "Pilgrim" cards (not permanent party members, only used this mission) gets shuffled into a mission zone. One flips face-up every turn — the campaign's first "start of turn" effect. Defeating an enemy costs the party reserve-deck discards equal to the combined strength of pilgrims sitting in that zone, though playing a card that matches a pilgrim's strength lets you banish it early, and an exact kill banishes one for free.
