# Evergreen Mode — Banes (1–23)

Transcribed from photographs of the physical cards (John, 2026-09-06). **Reference only — none of this
is implemented.** See [EVERGREEN-BOONS.md](EVERGREEN-BOONS.md) for the other half.

Banes are numbered **1–23** (23 cards). A published scenario code is one Location plus four banes plus
two boons, e.g. `C-4-8-15-19-25-32`.

Each card carries a timing band naming when it applies: **SETUP**, **SPECIAL RULES**,
**ENEMY DEFEATED**, or **END OF TURN**.

---

## 2 — Mental Health Day

> **SETUP** — Put 30 cards from top of reserve deck into the discard pile.

## 15 — Tread Carefully

> **ENEMY DEFEATED** — If you did not deal exact damage, move entire discard pile to the banished pile
> before carrying out the Location "Enemy Defeated" rules.
>
> Iconography: 💀 struck through (not an exact kill) = **D** → **B**.

## 23 — Choose Your Poison

> **END OF TURN** — You may discard a card from hand. If you don't, put the top card of the reserve
> deck into the mission zone.
>
> Iconography: hand → **D**, OR **R** → **MZ**.

## What these confirm about the mode

- **Locations carry their own "Enemy Defeated" rules**, which bane 15 modifies rather than replaces —
  so a Location is a rules card, not just a theme. The five are Shrine, Cave, Forest, Hills and Lake.
- **A mission zone exists in Evergreen Mode** (bane 23 puts cards into it), as in the campaign.
- The pile shorthand matches the campaign's own vocabulary: **R** reserve, **D** discard, **B**
  banished, **MZ** mission zone.
